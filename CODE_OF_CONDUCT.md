# Code of Conduct

We want participation in Compatibility Playbooks to be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving engineering practice.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful of differing experience, roles, and viewpoints.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, not personal attacks.
- Welcome questions and explain technical concerns with evidence where possible.
- Respect confidentiality and do not publish sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal information disclosure or threats of such disclosure.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or repeated abusive conduct.
- Posting secrets, customer data, or confidential incident details.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may close, restrict, or block participation for conduct that violates this policy. To report an issue, contact the repository owner through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This lightweight policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant community framework. All contributors are expected to follow it in project spaces.
